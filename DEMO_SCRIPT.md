# 3-minute live demo

Setup: `python main.py --empty --no-browser`, then open http://127.0.0.1:8000.

1. **The story (30 s, no screen).** "Three students are building a canteen ordering app. They make decisions in
   meetings. Six months later nobody remembers *why*. Memento collects the why as it happens."
2. **Play all (90 s).** The graph is empty. Press **Play all**. The chat plays, Memento reads each meeting,
   and dots pop in. Narrate: SQLite chosen and MySQL rejected, never store card details, SMS replaced by
   in-app alerts, staff must never see phone numbers, only college emails can sign up.
3. **Inspect (30 s).** Click the SQLite decision. The side panel shows why, the evidence, the rejected options,
   the assumption and what would change their mind. Tick/untick "show replaced decisions" to show the SMS plan.
4. **It keeps watching (20 s).** Open *Or paste a whole note*, press **Add pasted note**. The SQLite decision
   turns amber: under review.
5. **Capture your own (20 s).** Choose **Rule**, type one from the audience, press Add, then Save. A new dot appears.
